## Decisions and deviations

1. **Close delay.** Recovery waits 15 s (`buriedTabCloseDelay`) after a clean helper result
   before it treats a still-listed tab as dead and closes it: the helper lives up to about
   12 s after a clean result (its 10 s hold and the restore exchanges), so an immediate check
   would close a live tab in the middle of its own restore. A failure result is restored at
   once and left visible.
2. **Swept results** (older than D14's 120 s) are seen only through connection listings the
   coordinator makes anyway (the existing-attachment check, `detach(session:on:)`), and only
   when no connection was filtered out; the refresh engine's attachment checks are not wired
   in. After the review, a listing counts only against tabs watched before it started.
3. **`Configuration.buriesLaunchTabs`** (default on) is off in `e2e-bury`, whose spike buries
   by itself. **13c must turn it on** to run `e2e-bury` through the coordinator.
4. A bury and Show tab wait for each other in both directions; a refused restore ends Show tab
   with nothing activated.
5. A tab whose post-bury check saw its connection gone but has no result is restored at once
   and left visible, as for a swept result.
6. The self-tests do not assert the buried tab yet (13c's bullet).

## Review

Codex wrote `docs/reviews/phase-13b.md` (`autoclaude-review.sh`, 20 min budget) →
`ship-with-fixes`, 1 blocker, 2 should-fix. One review-fix worker fixed all three, each with a
regression that fails with its fix reverted:

- **Blocker — end evidence lost during an in-flight bury.** A `detach` taking the helper's
  result while the bury awaited its answer had it dropped (the tab was not registered yet); a
  failed restore after the bury then left a stranded buried tab. Now a result taken during the
  bury or its check is kept, and a connection loss the check saw is carried into the watch
  entry. Tests `aResultTakenDuringTheBuryIsKept(helperFailed:)`,
  `aConnectionLossSeenAfterTheBuryIsKept`.
- **Should-fix — stale ownership snapshots.** An attachment listing suspended while a new tab
  was buried could count that tab's connection as gone. Both call sites now record the watched
  tabs before the snapshot starts and apply absence only to those. Test
  `aCheckStartedBeforeABuryLeavesThatTabBuried` (a listing hold in `InstrumentedITerm2`).
- **Should-fix — e2e "left by itself" could be met by recovery.** The 15 s recovery `Close`
  could make the gateway vanish within the e2e wait. Every `E2ERun` coordinator now goes
  through `RecordingITerm2Integration`; `checkGatewayLeft` fails the step when any restore or
  `Close` that could touch that tab was recorded. Tests
  `forwardsEveryCallAndRecordsBuriesRestoresAndCloses`,
  `recordsTheCoordinatorsRecoveryOfADeadTab(helperRestored:)`. The probe has no test target, so
  the e2e use itself runs first in 13c.
