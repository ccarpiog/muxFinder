  confirmation drawn in the popover as Move here's; the confirmation's count is `clientCount`
  less one, with the "including <k> here" clause; result wording and recoveries; refresh rule;
  the hint in the existing attached-here text.
- The worker's (recorded in PLAN.md "Outcome of I-11b"): only the new item is disabled while
  another action is pending (the row's other items were never disabled; the model ignores
  them); both dialogs, Move here's included, now announce their title to VoiceOver; "Try again"
  refreshes the host when the item is no longer offered; `notAttachedHere` also forgets
  muxFinder's own record of that attachment.

  failure whose last action was a move here or a "Detach other clients…" (their confirmations
  are popover-only, D32); and `retry(session:)` does nothing for those two unless the popover
  is open and the switcher is not. Move here's identical pre-existing flaw is fixed with it.
