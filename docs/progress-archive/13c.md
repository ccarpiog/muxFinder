## Decisions and deviations

1. **`e2e-bury` now goes through the coordinator.** The spike's own bury, its direct `Close` of
   a dead tab, the re-buries emulating the default setting and the order sampler are gone.
   The spike cases (b: no `Disinter`, d: dead buried tab) became step 9, a real check of the
   coordinator's recovery. `Configuration.buriesLaunchTabs` stays as a switch, but no caller
   turns it off any more (D29 edited). `RecordingITerm2Integration.setBeforeBury(_:)` is the
   harness's timing hook (it also records each bury's answer); `checkLaunchTabLeft` in
   `E2ESupport.swift` is shared by `e2e-local`/`e2e-remote` and `e2e-bury`.
2. **Harness fix from the real runs.** The first `e2e-local` attempt failed step 7 ("found 1 of
   2" argv digests): since 13a the helper rewrites its digest after each tab restore, so a
   later step saw an earlier launch's rewrite. `takeNewArgvDigests()` in `E2ERun.swift` leaves
   out launches already checked (four call sites). No hermetic regression (the probe has no
   test target).
3. **iTerm2's auto-hide setting.** iTerm2 exited between 12:15 and 12:24 (not the run's doing;
   no crash report) and came back with its "Automatically bury the tmux client session" key
   absent, i.e. the default (off). So 13c's runs prove the **default-off** branch for real:
   iTerm2 buried none of the launch tabs itself; 13a's spike had run with it on. The 13a
   deviation (default branch emulated) is closed.
4. **Review fixes.** Step 7 now releases the bury and the detach together (the hook starts the
   bound `detach-client` in its own task and returns), requires a bury sent and answered `OK`
   with `burial` `buried` or `restoredAfterEnd`; an ownership refusal is its own OBSERVATION,
   never the race, and retries up to 3 attaches, then fails. Observed: Neptuno
   `restoredAfterEnd` (bury answered 10 ms, helper result 39 ms, first restore 40 ms), Saturno
   `buried` (bury 8 ms, helper restore 165 ms undid it). Step 11 asserts `childSignaled(9)` /
   exit 137, watches until 2 s past the helper's 10 s minimum lifetime, requires the tab in a
   tab at every look after it returns, the helper alive, and no coordinator `Close` or further
   bury; step 12 repeats the checks before closing. The pure judgements (`BuryRaceOutcome`,
   `KilledLaunchTabWatch`, `GatewayPlace`) live in `Sources/MuxFinderCore/Attach/
   LaunchTabChecks.swift` with `LaunchTabChecksTests` (8 tests, the negative cases).
5. **Real-run facts** (in `PLAN.md` *Verified facts*): step 9's tab stayed buried after tmux
   ended and stayed a dead buried session once its helper exited; the coordinator restored and
   closed it within 29 s of the helper's result. Step 11's tab came back after 889 ms locally
   and 2110 ms on Saturno. Detached tabs left by themselves 1–271 ms after the helper's result,
   about 5 s for detaches within 10 s. A buried session that never got `Disinter` answered the
   status report only after about 1 s locally, not within 2 s on Saturno.

## Open risks left

- The race's dangerous order (the bury landing after the helper's restore) was not forced for
  real: on both hosts the bury landed first. Forcing it needs a seam inside
  `ITerm2APIClient.buryLaunchTab` between its ownership check and the request; the order is
  covered by 13b's unit tests. Step 7's retry path ran only in unit tests.
- With the auto-hide setting **on** (13a's state), the 13c harness has not run; the spike
  covered that branch with the old harness.
- `CreatedSessionTests.killsOnlyTheRecordedSessionOnAPrivateServer` (6a) failed once while
  `scripts/build-app.sh` ran in parallel (a 12 s private-server wait under load); it passed
  in the full suite alone and 3/3 isolated. Another load-sensitive private-server test.
- `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/TESTING.md` still say "about 123 s" for `e2e-remote` (13c: 120.4 s).
- The user's own muxFinder.app had tmux attachments open during the runs; every check left
  them out.
