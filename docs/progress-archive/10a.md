## Decisions

- **D25** (new): the attach UX — per-session state machine and its seams, row actions and
  panels, `showLaunchTab(_:)`, the self-test design and safety rules, keep mode and its
  cleanup commands.
- **D22** edited: placeholders gone; `shutdown()` now cancels session actions and stops
  Settings, onboarding/ssh-setup, the queue, system events and the engine concurrently, waits
  for session actions at most 12 s and logs any still running; the quit reply deadline is 15 s
  (was 5 s), used by both app delegates.
- **D18** edited: `createAndAttach`'s created-session hook and the read-only
  `currentSession(named:on:)` lookup.

## Review

Codex → `ship-with-fixes`, 1 blocker, 3 should-fix — all fixed by a fix worker, each with a
regression test that failed with the fix reverted:

- **Blocker:** `shutdown()` awaited session actions before stopping the other subsystems, and
  `LaunchTab.open` shields `CreateTab` from cancellation, so the 5 s reply could fire first and
  cut off cleanup silently. Fixed by concurrent shutdown, a bounded 12 s wait with a log of what
  is left, and a 15 s deadline. Self-test cleanup detaches and kills nothing while an action is
  still running (a launch tab of unknown age may still attach) and prints what it left and the
  `tmux kill-session` to run.
- **New session progress lost:** updates of a newly created session were dropped (operation
  held only in `creationTask`). Fixed with the created-session hook.
- **Failed activation shown as focus:** now its own message with "Try again", attachment record
  kept.
- **Open existing could open a renamed session:** now a fresh exact-name lookup; no match says
  it ended or was renamed and opens nothing.

## Open risks

- A rename in the instant between the fresh listing and the attach is not caught (the attach's
  revalidation checks session id and creation time, not the name).
- "Open existing" lost its 20 s wait; the lookup's discovery timeout bounds it.
- The app's own Open uses the standard existing-attachment check, which asks every tmux
  connection; only the self-tests filter to their own launch tabs.
- Show tab is unverified against iTerm2 3.7.6.
- The self-tests need iTerm2 already running (setup never launches it) and wait until their
  launch tabs are 11 s old before killing their session.
- Quit can now take up to 15 s when an attach is in flight.
