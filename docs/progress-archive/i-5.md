## Acceptance criteria and evidence

- **One click activates.** Both switcher row kinds have a single `.onTapGesture` calling
  `AppModel.clickSwitcherItem(_:)` (`Sources/MuxFinderUI/Views/SwitcherView.swift`), which
  selects the row and activates it through `activateSwitcherItem(_:)`: a session row opens as
  Return does (D25), "New session…" shows the form as ⌘N does. `selectSwitcherItem(_:)` and
  both `onTapGesture(count: 2)` handlers are gone. — met (`aClickOnASessionOpensItAsReturnDoes`,
  `aClickOnNewSessionShowsTheFormAsCommandNDoes`).
- **A quick double click acts once.** A second Open on a row already opening was already
  ignored (D25's in-flight rule; `aSecondActivationOfARowOpeningStartsNothing`). One model bug
  found and fixed: activating "New session…" again while the form showed put the filter's
  name back over the user's edit; `newSessionFromSwitcher()` now does nothing while the form
  shows (`aSecondActivationOfNewSessionLeavesTheDraft`, fails with the fix reverted). Beyond
  the brief, needed for "show the form and then act on it": `SwitcherClickSequence`
  (`Model/AppModelSwitcher.swift`) and a local left-mouse monitor in
  `SwitcherWindowTrackingView` (`Views/SessionSwitcher.swift`) drop the rest of a click
  sequence (presses with click count above 1, their drags and release) in the switcher's
  window after a click activated a row; reset at each open and close; Return, ⌘N and the
  accessibility action never arm it. — met (`theRestOfAnActivatingClickIsIgnored`,
  `aRowActivatedOnThePressKeepsItsRelease`, `onlyAClickIgnoresTheRestOfItsSequence`).
- **The second click never reaches the window behind** (review fix). `SwitcherController.
  holdForClick()`, called by `clickSwitcherItem(_:)` first: until `NSEvent.doubleClickInterval`
  has passed (read through new `SwitcherSystem` seams `doubleClickInterval` and `runAfter`),
  the panel's content is never shorter than the panel was (`PopoverPanelSurface.
  minimumContentHeight`, the opaque background filling the rest; growth applies at once), and
  a close for the action's success, another app becoming active or the panel resigning key
  waits for the interval's end (the focus path activates iTerm2, which brings the last two at
  once). Esc, the shortcut, a press outside, a display change and the popover opening close at
  once and end the hold; an earlier hold's timer changes nothing once replaced. The engine
  still hears one opened and one closed. — met (`aClickHoldsAShrinkUntilTheIntervalEnds`,
  `growthDuringAHoldIsAppliedAtOnce`, `aSuccessCloseWaitsForTheHold`,
  `aReopenedSwitcherInheritsNoHold`, `onlyAClickHoldsThePanel`,
  `aFocusAfterAClickClosesOnceTheIntervalEnds`, `switcherRootIsNoShorterThanItsMinimum`; each
  fails with its part of the fix reverted, except the growth test, which passes on the old
  code by design and fails against a frame-freezing variant).
- **Everything else stays.** Hover, context menu, Return, ↑/↓, ⌘N, Esc, accessibility actions
  and hints (none mentioned a double click), pinned rows, failure carry-over, closing on
  success: no existing test's assertion changed; the popover tests' fake panel gained the new
  `minimumContentHeight` property only. — met.
- **Docs.** D32 *Keys* and a Phase I-5 section in `PLAN.md`; `README.md`; `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`;
  `docs/TESTING.md` (matrix row, Phase I-5 section); `docs/MANUAL-CHECKLIST.md` item 18 (one
  click on "New session…" shows the form, one click on a session opens it, a quick double
  click opens once, a quick double click on "New session…" under a long list shows the form
  once and clicks nothing behind the panel, and a double click on a session already open
  here). — met.
- **Gallery.** `--preview-gallery switcher --preview-snapshot` light and dark exit 0, look
  unchanged; `docs/screenshots/switcher-states-*.png` not regenerated. — met.
