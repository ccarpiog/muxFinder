## What happened

Runs 1 and 2 (11a tree) failed at step 8: iTerm2 3.7.6 copies every loaded dynamic profile into
its saved `New Bookmarks` (an entry with the generated GUID and a `Dynamic Profile Filename`
key, 78 keys) and drops it some 240–270 ms after the file goes. Diagnosed with a digest-only
comparison of `defaults export` (no value read; the 13 user entries were byte-identical). Fix
worker: the snapshot leaves out muxFinder's mirrors (`excludedMuxFinderEntries`) and step 13
waits for the mirror to go. Runs 3 and 4 passed. Codex review: two should-fix — the exclusion
was too loose (prefix + any path ending `muxFinder.json`), and step 13's 30 s was not an
absolute deadline. Review-fix worker: exclusion only for a well-formed generated GUID (the run's
exact GUID in the harness) whose `Dynamic Profile Filename` is exactly the snapshotter's own
`muxFinder.json` path and, in the harness, every key the run wrote unchanged; a baseline mirror
is waited for 5 s, else step 1 fails; step 13's deadline is absolute from the removal with
export timeouts and pauses capped, a late snapshot not counting. Runs 5 and 6 on the final tree
confirmed the strict rule against the real iTerm2.

## Decisions

- D26 and *Verified facts* record iTerm2's `New Bookmarks` mirroring; the acceptance text of
  Phase 11 notes that muxFinder's own mirrors are left out of the hash. muxFinder still never
  writes iTerm2 preferences: the mirror is iTerm2's own write.
- API: `ITerm2ProfileSnapshotter.Options` and `MirrorRule` replace `isMuxFinderMirror(_:)`;
  fingerprints compare with `hashesMatch` / `differences(from:)` (ignoring the excluded count).
