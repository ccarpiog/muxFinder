## Decisions

- The orchestrator's calls (*Phase I-10* in `PLAN.md`): one static script for this Mac and
  SSH hosts, not `NSMetadataQuery`; the channel asks folder-name questions only (the script
  builds the predicate); escaping and limits; end records that cannot be paths; the fixed
  noise rule (a)–(d) in the script; the channel's bounds; the scope preference in
  `preferences.json`; the probe.
- The worker's, recorded in D36: the script also refuses unescaped `"`, `*`, `?` and a stray
  `\` (`mdfind` silently accepts a predicate bent by them); the channel's script opens on the
  first searchable keystroke, before the debounce, so the connection is ready sooner; only an
  answer resets the failure count.
- The review fixes, recorded in D36: a 4 s host-side deadline per query (`mdfind` killed and
  reaped, answered `error timeout`, `searchTimedOut` in the channel, the script still usable),
  so the worst-case host-side lifetime after a disconnect is about 4 s (5 s if the answer
  stage never runs), because POSIX `sh` cannot watch stdin for EOF while a query runs without
  consuming query lines; the answer stage signals the `mdfind` stage once a 51st folder is
  kept; the transport's event queue holds at most 8 events, overflow reporting
  `eventOverflow`, ending the process, the end event still last.

## Open risks and deviations

- To verify the escaping against real `mdfind` by counts, the worker mounted a 16 MB scratch
  disk image of its own (hidden from Finder), turned Spotlight indexing on for it, queried
  synthetic folders on it, then detached and deleted it (no image from a temporary folder is
  mounted afterwards, checked with `hdiutil info`). That is more than the "read-only Spotlight
  queries" rule 3 names, though it touched no folder of the user's; noted for the user.
- For I-10b: the header's readability check (`~/Desktop`, `~/Documents`, `~/Downloads`) may
  make macOS ask for access the first time the app searches This Mac, so I-10b decides whether
  to skip it locally; paths containing a newline reach the parser's output and must pass
  `Validation.workingDirectory` before they are offered; a `'` in the middle of a word finds
  nothing (Spotlight's matching), worth a line in "Missing a folder?"; an unreachable host
  shows its problem after up to two connect timeouts (about 10 s).
- One full `swift test` run of the fix worker had 3 failures in `PopoverHeightLimitTests` (UI
  layout, untouched by this phase); they passed alone and in the next full runs, and in the
  orchestrator's final run.
- The fix worker ran one read-only `git diff --stat`, against its brief (nothing changed).

## Review

Codex wrote `docs/reviews/phase-I-10a.md` (`autoclaude-review.sh`, 20 min) →
`ship-with-fixes`, 1 blocker + 2 should-fix:

- BLOCKER, `FolderSearchTransport.swift`: closing an SSH channel did not end a blocked remote
  search → host-side per-query deadline (above); regression: a blocking stub `mdfind`, no stub
  process left after close or timeout.
- SHOULD-FIX, `FolderSearchScript.swift`: the limit relied on SIGPIPE and could stall while
  the filter consumed excluded paths → explicit stop of the `mdfind` stage; regression: a stub
  printing endless excluded paths after the 50th kept one.
- SHOULD-FIX, `FolderSearchTransport.swift`: unbounded `AsyncStream` → bounded queue with
  overflow failure; regression: a flood of valid replies with a stalled consumer.

Each regression failed with its fix reverted (fix worker), then passed.
